Paste your Apps Script web app URL to back up every entry to a Google Sheet automatically. How do I get this?
One-time setup (about 3 minutes):
  1. Create a blank Google Sheet at sheets.google.com.
  2. Go to Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete the starter code, paste in the script I gave you.
  4. Save it, then click Deploy → New deployment.
  5. Type: Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.
  6. Click Deploy, authorize it (click through the "unverified app" warning — it's your own script).
